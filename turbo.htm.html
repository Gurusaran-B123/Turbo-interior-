About TURBO

Fifteen years of turning wood into home

Based in Trichy, Tamil Nadu, TURBO Interior Design and Wood Works has spent 15 years designing and building interiors and wood works for homes. From a compact modular kitchen to a complete home interior, every piece is planned around your space, your budget and the way your family lives.

We work with plywood, solid wood and modern laminates and finishes, and we stay with your project from the first measurement to the final installation.

  • Modular kitchens, wardrobes, cupboards & pooja rooms
  • Staircases, plywood & wood doors, custom furniture
  • Complete home interiors — designed as one coordinated look
